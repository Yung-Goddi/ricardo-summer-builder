# 📓 How to Use Your Engineering Journal

*A one-minute guide. You've got this — no help needed.* 💪

---

## Every time you work on your project:

- [ ] ✅ **1. Open `dashboard.html`**
  Double-click it. Your home base opens in your browser.

- [ ] ⭐ **2. Read the "Start Here Next Time" box.**
  It tells you exactly where you left off. No more *"wait… where was I?"*

- [ ] 🛠️ **3. Build something.**
  This is the fun part. Make progress on your project.

- [ ] ✍️ **4. Before you stop, click "New Journal Entry."**

  Write:
  - 🔨 What did I build today?
  - 🧩 What problem did I solve?
  - 🛑 Where did I stop?
  - ➡️ What should I work on next?
  - 💡 Any new ideas or questions?

- [ ] 💾 **5. Save your work.**
  Hit **Save Entry**. Your ⭐ box updates itself for next time.

- [ ] 📋 **6. Click "Copy as Markdown" (or "Download .md").**
  Add it to your project so it becomes part of your **engineering portfolio** and **GitHub history**.

- [ ] 🚀 **7. Commit and push your work to GitHub.**
  ```bash
  git add . && git commit -m "Add journal entry" && git push
  ```

---

## 🤔 Why do this?

**Engineers don't just build — they keep records.**

Your journal helps you:

- 🧭 Remember where you left off.
- ⚡ Solve problems faster.
- 📈 Show your progress.
- 💼 Build a portfolio for future jobs, college, and projects.
- 🧑‍🏫 Teach others how you solved problems.

> Every great engineer leaves a trail others can learn from.
