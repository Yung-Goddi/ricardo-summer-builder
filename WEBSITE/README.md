# Website Phase

## Phase 3: Build a Real Website for Your Candy Business

---

Welcome to Phase 3. This is the biggest phase.

By the end of it, you'll have a real website, live on the internet, that people can actually visit to see your candy products and place orders.

Not a template. Not a builder like Wix or Squarespace. **Code you wrote yourself.**

---

## What You're Building

A 2-page website:

**Page 1: Home + Products** (`index.html`)
- Header with your business name
- List of your candy products with prices
- Short "About" section
- Navigation

**Page 2: Order Form** (`order.html`)
- Customer name and contact info
- Product selection and quantity
- Order submission that goes directly into your Google Sheets

Both pages will look good on phones too.

---

## Tech You'll Learn

**HTML** — The structure of every web page. HTML is like the skeleton.

**CSS** — How a page looks. Colors, fonts, layout. CSS is the skin and clothing.

**JavaScript** — How a page behaves. Clicks, forms, animations. JS is the muscles.

This is the entire foundation of the web. Every website you've ever visited uses these three things.

## The Connection to Google Sheets

When someone fills out your order form and clicks "Send Order," two things happen:

1. They see a confirmation message
2. Their order is automatically added to your Orders tab in Google Sheets

This works using **Google Apps Script** — a tool from Google that connects your website to your spreadsheet.

Setting this up is one of the coolest things in this program. It's real automation. You'll feel like a wizard when it works.

The setup is in `deploy_guide.md`.
