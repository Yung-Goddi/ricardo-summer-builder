# Screenshot and Demo Video Guide

## Capture Your Work

---

## Why This Matters

Code on a screen is invisible to most people.

A screenshot or a 3-minute video makes your work visible — to Dad, to future employers, to anyone you want to impress.

Capture things as you build them. Don't wait until the end.

---

## What to Screenshot

Screenshot EVERYTHING that works. Right when it works. Before you move on.

**micro:bit Projects:**
- Take a photo (not a screenshot) of your physical hardware setup with the micro:bit wired up
- If it has a display output — include that in the photo
- For the LCD project: photo of the LCD showing your business stats

**Google Sheets:**
- Dashboard tab: full screenshot
- Products tab: first 10 rows visible
- A chart you made

**Website:**
- Homepage on desktop (full browser screenshot)
- Homepage on mobile (use browser dev tools: F12 → toggle device toolbar)
- Order form
- Success message after submitting an order

**Game:**
- Game in progress (high score visible)
- Game over screen

## How to Screenshot

**Mac:**
- `Cmd+Shift+3` — Full screen
- `Cmd+Shift+4` — Drag to select area (best for specific windows)
- `Cmd+Shift+5` — More options + screen recording

**Windows:**
- `Win+Shift+S` — Select area (Snipping Tool)
- `PrtScn` — Full screen to clipboard
- `Win+G` — Xbox Game Bar (includes screen recording)

---

## How to Take a Good Hardware Photo

For micro:bit projects, a good photo shows:
1. The micro:bit clearly
2. The breadboard and wiring
3. The output (LEDs on, LCD showing text, etc.)

Tips:
- Good lighting — near a window or lamp
- Hold the phone/camera steady
- Get close enough that text on the LCD is readable
- Take 3 photos, use the best one

## Demo Videos

A demo video is 2–5 minutes. It shows something WORKING.

**Structure:**
1. (0:00–0:15) "Hi, I'm Ricardo. This is [project name]. I built this during my summer program."
2. (0:15–1:30) Show it working — do the thing live on camera
3. (1:30–2:30) "Here's one interesting thing about how I built it..." — explain a concept
4. (2:30–end) "Next I'm working on..." — forward-looking

**You don't need:**
- A script
- Perfect lighting
- To be polished
- To say "um" less

Just show the thing. Talk through it. Be real.

## Recording Your Screen

**Mac:** `Cmd+Shift+5` → "Record Selected Portion" or "Record Entire Screen"

**Windows:** `Win+G` → click the Record button (or `Win+Alt+R` to start)

For hardware demos, just point your phone at the setup.

## Where to Save Everything

In your GitHub repository:
```
portfolio/
├── screenshots/
│   ├── dashboard.png
│   ├── website_home.png
│   ├── website_mobile.png
│   ├── website_order.png
│   ├── game_playing.png
│   ├── game_over.png
│   └── microbit_lcd.jpg
└── demos/
    (put links to Google Drive or YouTube here, not the video files themselves — they're too large for GitHub)
```

For videos: upload to Google Drive, then add the shareable link to your portfolio README.

---

## The Friday Demo Rule

Every Friday (or whenever you finish a project), record a 2-minute video.

This habit does three things:
1. Forces you to verify it actually works
2. Builds your portfolio in real time
3. Gives you a record of your progress

Don't wait until the end of summer. Capture as you go.
