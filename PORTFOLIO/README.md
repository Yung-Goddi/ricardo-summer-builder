# Portfolio

## Show the World What You Built

---

Your portfolio is the proof.

It's the answer to "what did you do this summer?" that isn't just words.

By the end of summer, your GitHub repository will contain real projects — hardware, software, a business system, a website, a game. That's your portfolio. That's something you can show anyone.

---

## What Your Portfolio Will Contain

By the end of summer you should have:

### Code
- [ ] 6 micro:bit programs (in `microbit/` on GitHub)
- [ ] A candy business website (in `website/` on GitHub)
- [ ] A browser game (in `game/` on GitHub)
- [ ] Python automation scripts (in `bonus/` on GitHub)

### Documentation
- [ ] README.md at the root of your repository (describing everything)
- [ ] A journal covering 10 weeks of work
- [ ] Progress tracker showing what you completed

### Media
- [ ] Screenshots of every major project
- [ ] Photos of your hardware setups
- [ ] At least 2–3 demo videos

---

## The GitHub Repository README

Your repository's main `README.md` is the front door of your portfolio.

When someone visits your GitHub, this is the first thing they see.

It should include:
- What this repository is
- A summary of every project you built
- Links to the live website and game
- A photo or screenshot
- A "What I learned" section

Use `PORTFOLIO/portfolio_template.md` as a starting point.

## Screenshots

Capture screenshots of:
- Your Google Sheets dashboard
- Your website (both pages)
- The game running
- The LCD display with your business stats

How to screenshot:
- Mac: `Cmd+Shift+4` (drag to select area)
- Windows: `Win+Shift+S` (Snipping Tool)

Save screenshots in `portfolio/screenshots/` in your GitHub repository.

---

## Demo Videos

A demo video is a 2–5 minute screen recording where you:
1. Say your name and what you built
2. Show it working live
3. Explain one interesting thing about how it works

For hardware demos: point your phone at the setup and narrate.

**How to record your screen:**
- Mac: `Cmd+Shift+5` → Screen recording
- Windows: `Win+G` → Xbox Game Bar → Record

Name your videos clearly: `demo_microbit_game.mp4`, `demo_candy_website.mp4`

Upload them to Google Drive and add the links to your README.
