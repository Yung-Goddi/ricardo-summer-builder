## How This Fits With Your Other Files

You have a few files. Here's the one-line version so you never have to guess:

| File | When you use it | What it's for |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **WORK_LOG.md** (this file) | **Every session** | Your daily notes, questions, and where to restart |
| `JOURNAL/weekN.md` | **Once a week** | Deeper reflection — what you *learned*. This is portfolio material. |
