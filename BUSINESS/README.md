## Why Google Sheets (Not Excel)

You already had an Excel workbook. We're not throwing it away — keep it as a backup.

We're switching to Google Sheets because:

1. **It lives in the cloud.** No more "where's the file?" It's always there on any device.
2. **Dad can see it.** He doesn't need to ask you how business is going — he can look.
3. **It connects to Google Forms.** Your website can send orders directly into Sheets.
4. **It's free.** And you already have a Google account.

Excel → Google Sheets is a pretty easy transition. The formulas are almost identical.

## The Final System

When you're done, your system will look like this:

```
Google Sheets:
├── Tab: Inventory      (what you have, quantities, costs)
├── Tab: Sales Log      (every sale you make)
├── Tab: Orders         (orders from your website)
├── Tab: Products       (your full product list with prices)
└── Tab: Dashboard      (charts, totals, summary)
```

The Orders tab will be automatically filled in by your website. That connection is built in the Website phase.
